# Content Factory Publish Quality Gate

A video is publishable only if all mandatory checks pass.

## Story
- Hook creates curiosity within first 2 seconds.
- Every factual claim is traceable to research sources.
- Script contains a payoff, not just a fact dump.
- No fabricated quotes or misleading certainty.

## Visual
- 9:16, 1080x1920 target.
- Meaningful visual change every 1.5–3 seconds.
- No static slideshow sequence longer than 4 seconds.
- Captions remain readable inside platform safe areas.
- Visuals support the spoken claim rather than acting as generic filler.

## Audio
- Natural Arabic narration.
- Speech remains intelligible on phone speakers.
- Music must sit below narration and never mask words.
- SFX used selectively for hook, transitions, and payoff.

## Retention
- Remove greetings and intros.
- First payoff/novel information before second 8.
- Pattern interrupt around seconds 8–12 and 25–35.
- Ending must deliver the promised insight before CTA.

## Monetization safety
- Original narration and editorial structure.
- No compilation/re-upload workflow.
- Avoid mass-producing near-identical scripts or visuals.
- Keep source/evidence package with every story.

## Decision
- PASS: publish candidate.
- REWORK: fix identified issue and rerender.
- REJECT: factual weakness, weak hook, or insufficient originality.
