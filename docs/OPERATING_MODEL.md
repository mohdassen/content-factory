# Content Factory Operating Model

## Daily loop

1. Collect 20 candidate ideas.
2. Reject unverifiable or low-value claims.
3. Score ideas from 0-100.
4. Research the top 5 with at least two credible sources.
5. Produce the top 3 into short-form packages.
6. Render and publish platform-native versions.
7. Record retention, completion, shares, comments, follows, and revenue signals.
8. Generate follow-up ideas from winners, not from arbitrary templates.

## Initial publishing target

- 3 unique shorts per day.
- Arabic-first.
- One source-backed story per video.
- Prefer 35-55 seconds; use 60-90 seconds when the story benefits from depth or platform monetization rules favor it.

## Winner rules

A video is a candidate winner when it materially outperforms the channel median on at least two of:

- 3-second hold rate
- average percentage viewed
- completion rate
- share rate
- comments per 1,000 views
- followers per 1,000 views

The system should create new angles around the winning topic or narrative mechanic, not duplicate the same video.

## Monetization order

1. Audience and distribution proof.
2. Relevant affiliate offers.
3. Sponsors.
4. Digital products/newsletter/community when audience intent is clear.
5. Platform ad revenue as an additional stream, not the sole business model.
