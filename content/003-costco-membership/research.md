# Research Pack — Costco Membership Economics

## Verified data
Costco reported FY2025 net sales of $269.912B and membership fees of $5.323B. Membership fees are therefore small relative to merchandise sales, but they are a strategically important recurring revenue stream. Costco is membership-only and its U.S. Gold Star membership is currently $65/year.

## Editorial accuracy
Do NOT say 'Costco makes all its profit from memberships.' That is an attractive but misleading simplification. Use the more defensible angle: the membership model gives Costco billions in recurring fee revenue and reinforces loyalty, while the warehouse model focuses on high sales volumes and value.
