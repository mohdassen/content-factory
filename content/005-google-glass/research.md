# Research Pack — Google Glass

## Core story
Google Glass is a strong example of a technologically ambitious product that reached the market before the product-market fit, social norms, privacy expectations, price, and use cases were mature enough for broad consumer adoption.

## Editorial framing
Do not frame Glass as proof that wearable AR itself was a bad idea. The stronger story is that technically impressive products can fail commercially when customers do not receive enough everyday value to compensate for price, friction, privacy concerns, and social discomfort.

## Fact-safety
- Avoid invented claims about exact total losses unless supported by company filings.
- Distinguish the early consumer Explorer era from later enterprise versions.
- Do not say Google permanently abandoned smart glasses as a category.

## Short angle
Hook: "Google had the money, engineers and hype. So why did people still not want Glass?"
Lesson: technology strength is not the same as product-market fit.
