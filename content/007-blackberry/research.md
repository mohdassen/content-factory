# 007 — BlackBerry

## Story angle
How a dominant smartphone brand became trapped by the strengths that originally made it successful.

## Production strategy
- Preserve V3 Golden / Video 006 stable pipeline.
- FLUX.1-schnell independent portrait scene masters.
- No baked text, captions, logos, numbers, UI labels, or watermarks in generated imagery.
- Subtitles remain a post-render layer.
- Preserve cinematic brand logo and 2.6s outro.
- Mandatory technical QC before Telegram delivery.

## Experimental improvements for 007
Only presentation-layer improvements are allowed; do not alter the stable generation/delivery architecture.
- Strong visual hook in first 2–3 seconds.
- Gentle cinematic pan/zoom motion per scene.
- Short restrained transitions where safe.
- Improve subtitle emphasis without changing narration synchronization.
- Keep rollback path to Video 006 baseline if any experiment fails.

## Visual direction
Eight independent 9:16 cinematic scenes: early executive smartphone era, tactile keyboard close-up, obsessive mobile-email culture, disruptive touchscreen arrival, incumbent confidence, app-centric smartphone shift, fading former market leader, reflective final image contrasting old keyboard silhouette with modern touchscreen glow. Avoid readable trademarks/UI text in generated scenes.
