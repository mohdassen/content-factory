# Research Pack — NVIDIA's Long CUDA Bet

## Verified backbone
NVIDIA was founded in 1993. Its own investor history identifies 2006 as the year it unveiled CUDA, expanding GPU use into scientific computing. NVIDIA describes its evolution as moving from a chip vendor toward a computing platform. Long before the current generative-AI boom, CUDA and the surrounding developer/software ecosystem expanded GPU computing beyond graphics. NVIDIA's current business now centers heavily on accelerated computing and AI infrastructure.

## Editorial angle
Avoid claiming CUDA alone 'caused NVIDIA to become an AI giant.' The stronger story is compounding platform investment: hardware + CUDA + libraries + developer ecosystem created an advantage that became exceptionally valuable as AI workloads accelerated.
