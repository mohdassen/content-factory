# 006 — Apple Newton

Topic: Apple Newton MessagePad and why an early personal digital assistant failed before smartphones became mainstream.

Core facts for production:
- Apple introduced the Newton MessagePad in 1993.
- It was a PDA platform centered on pen input, notes, calendar/contact functions and handwriting recognition.
- Early handwriting recognition became a prominent weakness and subject of public criticism/parody.
- Newton products were discontinued in 1998 after Steve Jobs returned to Apple.
- The iPhone launched in 2007, years after Newton.

Story angle: being early is not enough; product timing and technical readiness matter.
